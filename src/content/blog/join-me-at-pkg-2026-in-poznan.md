The Poznań Economic Congress, organized by Instytut Poznański, takes place on November 12 and 13.

This year, I will be taking an active part in the program, leading a workshop and moderating an expert panel.

## Practical workshop: “From ChatGPT to agents: how to build an AI strategy for your company”

We will discuss how companies can move beyond basic AI tool use and start building a thoughtful strategy for adopting artificial intelligence across their organizations.

**Friday, November 13, at 1:00 p.m.**

## Expert panel: “AI in business: how artificial intelligence is changing productivity, company structures, and competitive advantage”

I will have the pleasure of moderating a discussion on the impact of artificial intelligence on business operations, productivity, and competitiveness.

**Friday, November 13, at 3:00 p.m.**

I look forward to interesting conversations and the opportunity to share experiences. Admission to the Congress is free.

Program and registration: [kongres.poznanski.org](https://kongres.poznanski.org/)

See you in Poznań!
