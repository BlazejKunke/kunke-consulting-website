Imagine a potential client who no longer types “accounting firm Poznań” into Google. Instead, they ask ChatGPT: “I’m looking for an accountant in Poznań who handles limited liability companies, replies to emails within a day and isn’t the most expensive in town.” They get three names, each with a short explanation. Is your company among them? If so, how is it described? With your current price list, or the one from two years ago that someone once posted on a forum?

Until recently, businesses had practically no influence over this. On 10 November, OpenAI announced Businesses in ChatGPT, a new feature in beta. According to OpenAI, 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT every week. This is no longer a toy for enthusiasts. It is a channel where customers search, compare and make decisions.

## What exactly was announced?

In short, three things:

1. **Business Hub.** A panel where you can claim your company’s profile in ChatGPT, verify it and correct the basic information. Shops can add their own product catalog with prices and availability.
2. **Insights.** How many times ChatGPT showed your company, how many clicks the link to your website received and which conversation topics your company is associated with.
3. **Business Agents.** Your company’s AI assistant running inside ChatGPT. It answers customers’ questions about your offer and, when a customer is ready to buy, sends them to your website.

For now, all of this is free.

## Does it work in Poland?

Partly.

Companies from countries where ChatGPT is available can claim a profile in Business Hub. However, OpenAI has not published a list of countries, so the simplest thing is to check for yourself. Verification is done by text message or a phone call to the company’s main number, or through a DNS record on your domain. A Shopify store is not required.

At launch, AI agents are available only to selected online shops and online service businesses in the United States. For most Polish companies, this is something to keep an eye on for now.

## Will a profile improve my position in ChatGPT?

OpenAI says no. Claiming a profile on its own is not supposed to change how often a company appears in answers, because ChatGPT is meant to stay objective. Better, up to date information may still help you show up in the right conversations. Your website still matters a great deal.

Here I’ll allow myself a little skepticism. This reminds me of the launch of Google’s business listings: free, simple, “doesn’t affect rankings.” Today it is one of the most important tools in local marketing and a significant part of Google’s advertising business.

With ChatGPT, the direction is visible from day one. A company’s agent can already be sponsored and used as the destination for ads in ChatGPT. OpenAI says so openly: an organic version and a paid version, the same agent. An OpenAI representative also did not rule out that Business Hub may become paid at some point. The business model is easy to read: first build a directory of businesses, then sell visibility.

That is not a criticism. It is simply worth knowing before you build a strategy on it.
